An introductory computing course that integrated socially responsible computing throughout the curriculum. This course informed several publications, including work on [socially responsible computing in introductory courses](https://doi.org/10.1145/3626252.3630926) and a [multi-institutional study at Hispanic-Serving Institutions](https://doi.org/10.1145/3708521).
